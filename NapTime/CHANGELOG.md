# Changelog

## 0.2.0 (2026-10-12)

- **Naps in your own world**: in a world your own game runs (playing alone, or the game's Start Server), your bed works by day too, with no server mod needed: once everyone is in bed in the daytime, the clock runs on to sunset and everyone wakes at dusk. New settings: **Naps in your own world** (on), and its hours and afternoon rule (from sunrise to sunset, each player picking in the afternoon, as Nap Time (Server)'s out of the box). Friends who join with the pack can nap with you. With miikeskii's Server-Side QoL Pack also in your game, its Nap Time (Server) decides instead.
- **A line under the Enabled switch** in the settings window says what applies where you are: this server runs naps (and its hours), it doesn't, or your own world and who decides there.

## 0.1.0 (2026-10-11)

- **First version** (his name, Nap Time): the players' half of Nap Time (Server). On a server whose miikeskii's Server-Side QoL Pack runs Nap Time (Server), your own bed works by day inside the server's nap window (sunrise to sunset out of the box), and once everyone is in bed the clock runs on to sunset. In the afternoon, out of the box, E sleeps to the morning as the game does and Left Alt + E naps until dusk; each key's choice is a setting. The bed's hover text says what each key does, and a nap ends with "Good evening". Anywhere else, the game's own bed.
