# Changelog

## 0.2.0 (2026-10-12)

- **Your own world's day**: in a world your own game runs (playing alone, or the game's Start Server), new settings set the length of the day and of the night, how long each weather lasts and how long the wind holds, as World Settings (Server) does on a server, with no server mod needed. Each is 0, the game's own, out of the box. Friends who join with the pack follow it. With miikeskii's Server-Side QoL Pack also in your game, its World Settings (Server) decides instead.
- **A line under the Enabled switch** in the settings window says what applies where you are: this server sets the day (and to what), it doesn't, or your own world and who decides there.

## 0.1.0 (2026-10-11)

- **First version**: the players' half of World Settings (Server). On a server whose miikeskii's Server-Side QoL Pack runs World Settings (Server), your game's day follows the server's: the length of a day and of a night, how long each weather lasts and how long the wind holds; the sky is set straight to the right time of day. Anywhere else, the game's own.
