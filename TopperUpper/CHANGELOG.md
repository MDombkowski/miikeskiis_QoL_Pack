- Two settings' descriptions shortened: "Lights and stations a friend's game without this mod looks after" and "Stand aside while the server feeds a station". Nothing else changed.

## 0.1.0 (2026-10-09)

- **First version**, as a mod of miikeskii's QoL Pack and on its own (card A77, his name for it). The pack's own replacement for the server's ServersideQoL feeding, run by each player's own game.
- **Lights and fires** (on): torches, sconces, braziers, fires, the hearth, the hot tub and the rest near you are topped up with their fuel from the chests near you; a switch per kind; fires the world made and resin candles off; your reserves kept ("Resin:50" out of the box); the emptiest first when fuel is short.
- **Stations** (off until the server's feeding is switched off): smelters, the blast furnace, the charcoal kiln, the windmill, the spinning wheel, the eitr refinery, the Frigid Kiln, the fermenter (filled and tapped), the shield generator and the Frost Foundry's fuel, fed from the chests near each by your rules: what each takes first, the kiln's woods (Wood alone), reserves (seed stock kept), maximums that count what's queued and lying at the outputs, leave-one, fuel only while there's something to process. The stone oven's fuel, the ballista and harvesting beehives, birds' nests and sap extractors are there, off.
- **Leave one alone**: Left Alt + E at a light or station switches it between fed and left alone, saved on it.
- **Linked chests**: Left Alt + J at a station, then E on a chest for an input and Left Shift + E for an output; saved on the station by the chests' places; they glow while you point at the station. A station with linked inputs takes only from them.
- **Products** put away into the linked output chest, or into chests near the station like the quick stack (chest setups and others' marked chests followed), a second after they land; the windmill's early empty (off).
- Only the game that looks after a light or station feeds it, from chests that game already holds: nothing is ever claimed, and no ownership is ever asked for.
- **A feed is one write**: as the station's owner, what a feed takes is written straight into it the way the game's own add writes it, with the game's added effect once per feed: no log line per item, however much goes in. A friend's station fed as a hand would still gets one message per item.
- **Never the Obliterator**: its container is never counted, taken from, put into or linked (what goes in is destroyed at its next pull).
- In the pack, nothing is taken from (or put into) a chest a craft's bar is counting on (on its own, it can't see Craft from Chests' crafts); one count of what every feed took is shared by lights and stations, so together they never go below a reserve; a fermenter's meads count while they're on their way out; a station farther than the count range isn't fed past a maximum it can't count; a spinning wheel without a roof isn't fed; a fire, an oven or a Frost Foundry its owner's game has just loaded is fed only once it has counted the time it was away; Left Alt + E on a chest while linking stays Reserve Stock's.
- **Loose items** (off until the server's auto pickup is switched off): items your own game looks after, a few seconds after they're made, lying still (or floating) and away from players, go into chests within 64 m that already hold them (or are set up for them), following chest setups and others' marks; a whole stone deposit in a few frames (at most 40 items and 4 chests a frame), one line per chest ("Stone x87"), never an ownership request, a claim or a log line per item, never the Obliterator. Left where they lie: what you dropped on purpose, food near a tame that eats it, an egg lying alone, a swimming fish, what lies at a fed station's output, and the kinds in "Never pick up". The server still picking up items is said once, in the log and on screen. An error in it switches off only the pickup.
- **Skip the log line for a repeat ownership request** (on): asks for an item this game no longer looks after are skipped without the game's lines, with one line a minute counting them; a handover is exactly the game's own. Switch it off for the freeze tests.
