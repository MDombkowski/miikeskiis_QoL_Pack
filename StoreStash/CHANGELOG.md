# Changelog

## 0.7.0 (2026-10-09)

- **Personal chests** (card A80, miikeskii's feedback of 2026-10-09): a person button in every chest's window, under Reserve Stock's padlock, marks the chest as yours or takes your mark off (as Left Alt + H); gold while it's yours. A new setting, **Other players at my marked chests**: open as usual (out of the box), look but don't touch, or keep them out. Friends on the pack follow it in their own games, and with **Their mods follow it too** (on) their quick stack, Craft from Chests, Add to Cart and Outfitter keep out too. Pointing at a friend's marked chest says when you may only look, or are kept out; at such a chest the sort, setup, tidy and trash can leave it alone, and while its window is open your quick stack and one stack don't put into it. A friend can't hammer down a marked chest they may only look at, or are kept out of, while it holds anything. Friends without the pack, or with an older one, aren't stopped.
- **The Obliterator is never a chest**: the quick stack, Stack Overflow's tidy and every other mod of the pack leave it alone, so coal can no longer be put away into an obliterator and destroyed at the next pull of its lever. It can't be marked as anyone's; an old mark comes off with the key or the button.
- **Chest setups** (card A81): a group can come from biomes of its own: the small button beside a heading's Take all (or on a page's "All of" line) opens a row of biomes, so a chest can take Mistlands Weapons beside Food from every biome; the From: row narrows the rest, as before. **Quick picks**: Metals' Metal Ingots and Metal Ores and Scrap; Food's Prepared Dishes, Cooked Meat and Fish, Raw Meat and Fish, and Health, Stamina, Eitr and Balanced Foods (as the game colours them); Meads and Potions' Finished Meads: each one click, and named so in what the chest takes. New setting: **Quick picks on a family's page** (on). Setups already in your chests read exactly as before; a setup that leaves a quick pick out is written so an older pack takes nothing into that chest rather than what it leaves out.
- **Trophies by their biome** (card A82): "Trophies" has two new choices: a trophy goes to a chest holding trophies from the same biome, else to any chest holding a trophy (SameBiomeTrophyChest); or only to its biome's trophy chest, else it stays with you (SameBiomeTrophyChestOnly). Out of the box nothing changes.
- **One stack by a click** (card A82): with your inventory open, middle-click a stack to put away just that stack by the quick stack's rules, even a favourite or a stack on your hotbar, leaving your other stacks of it with you. New settings: **One stack: key or button** (the middle mouse button) and **One stack while a chest is open** (on).
- **Sorting merges favourite stacks** (card A82, at miikeskii's word): a favourite's stacks merge into the first of them. New setting in Sorting: **Merge favorite stacks** (on); off keeps them apart, as before.
- **Messages fit the screen** (card A82): the note the first time a quick stack takes from your hotbar is on three short lines (it ran off both sides of the screen), and every StoreStash message is broken into lines that fit.
- **F1 in a fold per feature** (card A82): Quick stack (with Which chests, What goes where, One stack by a click, Place stacks and holding E, Messages and sounds), Favorites, Personal quick stack, Personal chests, Sorting, Trash, Chest setups and Stack Overflow. Your settings file keeps every setting where it was.
- The chest service and the catalogue it carries changed (above, and Add to Cart 0.5.0's leave-one of gear).

## 0.6.3 (2026-10-08)

- **Skarif's Valheim Performance Overhaul no longer leaves chests stuck** (card A69, miikeskii's report of 2026-10-08: building from chests 50 to 60 m away kept failing on his friend's server, and much less with Skarif's mod off). That mod makes your game send its updates to the server nearest first, every time, so in a busy base the news that a chest changed hands could wait for minutes, and your game and the server each thought the other had it. The chest service now switches off just that part of Skarif's mod while the game runs: a new section in F1, **Performance mods (every mod)**, with **Skarif's mod: send in the game's own order** (on). Nothing changes without Skarif's mod.
- **"Beyond the server's reach" only when it is**, in the log's line for a chest that couldn't be had: a chest your game claimed itself because the server hadn't handed it over (55 m away) was called beyond the server's reach.

## 0.6.2 (2026-10-08)

- **One place for the chest settings every chest mod must agree on** (card A69): a new section in F1, **Chests (every mod)**, holds the chest range (up to 250 m now, 150 out of the box), **Chests beyond the server's reach** (now **on** out of the box, one switch for Add to Cart, Craft from Chests, StoreStash and Outfitter instead of four) and **Write chest problems to the log** (on). This mod's own "Chest range" is 0 out of the box, which follows the shared one; set it to give this mod a range of its own. A settings file still at the old 150 moves to 0 once. Its search radius can now reach 250 m, and so can Stack Overflow's tidy radius.
- A quick stack that had to leave chests out says how many weren't handed to you in time, or were beyond the server's reach; the log says why for each.
- **The chest service it carries changed** (card A69): a chest your game can't get at all is left out instead of being asked for and timing out; Far Fetched on the server (card A70, a separate server mod) is recognised, and chests it hands you are used at once; a far chest is never claimed where the server would hand it out itself.

## 0.6.1 (2026-10-06)

- **miikeskii's own settings are the defaults now** (card A63): "Light up the chests" (the chests a quick stack put something into glow for two seconds), the personal quick stack's button beside the inventory, the setup button beside a chest's window, and "Say where things went" for set-up chests are on out of the box.
- An older settings file moves to the new defaults once, only where it still holds the old default: a setting changed by hand stays as it is (the kit's new `ModuleSettings.MovedDefault`, remembered in a hidden setting, `DefaultsMoved`). The log names what moved.
- **Chests beyond the server's hand-out work now** (card A64): on a server, the game hands a chest only to a player within about 64 to 96 m of it, so a chest loaded farther out with nobody near it never answered ("owners didn't answer in time"). The chest service it carries now claims such a chest itself, as the game's own portals do: only one no game can answer for (nobody's, its owner gone from the game, or a dedicated server's far from the world's centre), never a cart's or a ship's; it changes it only once the server's newest copy has come in and between the server's two-second rounds, so such a chest can take a second or two longer than a near one, and it lets it go again right after. It is **off out of the box**: switch on the new setting "Chests beyond the server's reach" to use it (on your own server first). Alone or hosting, and within your own area, nothing changes; on a very slow connection such chests are left out, as before. One rare case stays, written in the chest service: with ServersideQoL on the server, that mod writing the same far chest in the instant your change is late reaching the server could copy or lose that one change. Nothing depends on a performance mod: with or without Skarif's ValheimPerformanceOverhaul (which never touches chests) it works the same.

## 0.6.0 (2026-10-05)

- **Stack Overflow** (card A42, miikeskii's idea, built into StoreStash at his pick): chests set up alike act as partitions of one chest.
- **At quick stack** a thing already goes to the set-up chest holding the most of it, then on into the next when that one is full ("Which chest first"'s rule "...the one holding the most of it", on by default): nothing changes there.
- **The tidy:** **Left Alt + N**, pointing at a set-up chest or with its window open, moves things between the chests set up like it so each kind sits in as few of them as possible: a kind already all in one chest stays there (unless a bigger kind needs its room and splitting it leaves your kinds in fewer chests in all), one spread over several goes to the chest that keeps it (by default the one already holding the most of it), one too big for any chest fills the roomiest first, and other things move out of its way where they must go (his boar pelts example: 8 stacks of Leather Scraps in one chest, 3 in another, the 3 go into the chest with 8). When every chest is full, one stack waits in a chest with a free cell while the rest swap; with none free, it stops and says so. At most 500 stacks a press; a bigger tidy says to press again. Chests Craft from Chests is holding for a craft under way are left out. Each chest it changed is then sorted as a chest is sorted as it opens. A button in the chest window does the same (off at first).
- **Safe with friends:** the chests a tidy changes are asked of their owners' games first, read again once handed over and planned again, and each move goes into the target first, measured, then out of the source, through the chest service's move (unchanged). A chest someone has open is left out that time, and said.
- **Its own section, "Stack Overflow"**, with twenty-five settings: the key and the button; what a tidy covers (the chest's group, or every group in reach); ask first above so many stacks; alike means identical, or ignoring biomes; which chest keeps a kind (the one holding the most, the biggest, the nearest); moving other things out of the way; merging part-stacks between chests; free cells left in each chest, and whether to keep them even if a kind splits (off: a kind is never split for them); things no setup takes left where they are or gathered; sorting the changed chests; the most stacks per tidy; a radius of its own; carts' and ships' chests (both off); chests others marked left alone; tidying after each quick stack, or every so many minutes (both off); what it says; the sound; the light-up; a key hint on set-up chests. Out of the box nothing changes until you press the key.
- **In F1** Stack Overflow folds under StoreStash's heading after Chest setups, its settings in five groups.

## 0.5.0 (2026-10-05)

- **miikeskii's first look at chest setups** ("working pretty great so far"), seven points (card A41, decisions 22 to 29).
- **Which chest first** (the quick stack's own settings, under What goes where): the three switches "Set-up chests first", "The most specific setup first" and "The chest holding most of it first" become one list you drag (or move with Up and Down), each rule with its own box: Chests set up for it, ...the most specific setup, ...the one holding the most of it, and **My personal chests** (new, off: the ones Personal Stacker counts as yours). The first rule that tells two chests apart decides, and the chest it puts first gets all it can take before the next one is touched; chests no rule tells apart go as before. The two "..." rules sort set-up chests among themselves, so they stay under the first. Your three switches are carried over once. Within the set-up chests, a more specific one (or one holding more) now fills before a lesser one's part-stacks are topped up, and a set-up chest's room goes to what its setup takes before what it merely holds (with "A set-up chest also takes what it holds" on). Coming from StoreStash 0.4.0's own release into the pack, set Which chest first again if you had changed those three switches: the pack's settings file never had them.
- **Biomes pair with families in either order:** with biomes alone ("everything from the Swamp and Plains biomes"), a family's button reads **Only this** and pairs the family with those biomes, so the Swamp, the Plains, then Trophies give Swamp and Plains trophies, as Trophies first does. A **right-click** on a heading's button leaves its group out. Trophies' and Creature Parts' biome headings can be taken on their own (Swamp Trophies). The biome row says "From:", and the top line says what the chest takes in words ("Takes Trophies, only from the Swamp and Plains biomes, and nothing else."); the hover line says it shorter ("Takes: Trophies from Swamp, Plains").
- **Search:** a box over the list on the left, as in Add to Cart: every word must match a thing's name, family, member, kind or biome ("swamp troph"); while it shows only part of a group, its heading's button names the whole group ("Take all Trophies"). While a box in the window has the keyboard, E, Tab and Esc don't close the inventory, and StoreStash's keys stand aside.
- **Marks:** a green check on everything the chest takes; pointing at it turns it into a red X, which takes the thing out (unpicked, or left out of its family); a right-click on a thumbnail does the same. In "This chest's setup", a left click elsewhere on a thumbnail does nothing. A thing left out shows a red minus, which takes it back.
- **The chest's name** in the window's title, or its kind while it has none: click it, type, Enter; Esc keeps the old one, an empty name clears it. Kept where MyLittleUI keeps chest names (up to 32 characters), so both show it; a setting (on) switches renaming off.
- **"Say where things went"** now takes the quick stack line's place: everything into set-up chests, only where it went ("Into set-up chests: 20 Wood → Wood chest", a line per chest); some into other chests too, "15 items into 2 other chests" first and the set-up lines below, in one message. A named chest is called by its name. Each line after the first keeps the message fully shown a second longer ("Seconds per extra line"), at most 10 seconds on screen ("Longest on screen"); a one-line message fades as before.
- **In F1** each feature's settings sit in groups, each behind a row of its own ("[+] Copy and paste"), and the quick stack's own fold into four under its first few.
- **Also:** a right-click on a heading that reads "Biome not picked" never turns a chest into "everything from its biomes" (it says why instead; a left click there still unpicks it and says the chest now takes everything from them); a heading's button reads "Take", not "Take all", while biomes narrow it; a chest's name, and a setup's words, written by another game are shown as plain text (no tags and no "<", no "$" words the game would translate, a line break as a space, no invisible characters), and a name typed in the window is kept as it shows (one that would show as nothing leaves the name as it was, and says why); "Nothing picked yet" shows only for a chest with nothing picked; dragging "Chests set up for it" never swaps the two "..." rows under it; and a "Which chest first" written by hand in the file is put right at start (unless it names a rule from a newer version).
- New settings: Which chest first, Seconds per extra line, Longest on screen (seconds), Rename the chest in the setup window, and a row for each group; gone: the three switches (carried over). The chest service (in every mod that carries it) orders a quick stack's chests by the list (`StackRules.Order`, `StackRules.Personal`, `ChestOrder`).

## 0.4.0 (2026-09-30)

- **Chest setups** (card A41, miikeskii's idea "Stack Allow List", built into StoreStash at his pick): a chest can be set up to take only the kinds of things you pick, from the catalogue Add to Cart's Take tab browses: whole families (Creature Parts), their members (Hides and Pelts), Gear's kinds (Arrows), single things, things left out ("Creature Parts, except Needle"), and biomes, which narrow the families, members and kinds you pick ("Hides and Pelts · Plains"); biomes with none of those picked take everything from them, and a thing picked by name comes from any biome.
- **The quick stack follows setups:** a set-up chest takes what its setup takes even when it holds none yet, and nothing else, even what it already holds (a setting: more of what it holds too). Set-up chests fill before the rest, part-stacks first within each: the most specific setup first (a thing, a Gear kind, a member, a family, biomes alone), then the chest holding the most, then the nearest (each a setting). What they can't fit goes on to other chests, and what no setup takes goes where it did before (two settings, both on). A set-up chest someone has open, or whose owner's game doesn't answer, or that's full, stays the home of what it takes, so with "What set-up chests can't fit goes on" off that stays with you. P and H follow setups; so do Place stacks and holding E at a set-up chest (a setting, on).
- **The setup window**, over the crafting panel beside the chest's: the families down the left, Take all on each heading, a click on a thing to take it or leave it out, a row of biomes, a line saying what the chest takes. Opened by **Left Alt + J** pointing at a chest (it opens the chest the game's own way) or **a tag button** in the chest window (off at first); every change is saved into the chest at once.
- **Copy and paste:** Ctrl+C / Ctrl+V while a chest's window is open, or the window's buttons; pasting replaces the setup (a setting: adds to it), and says what the chest now takes; a setting puts it on the clipboard as text too, to share. The setup keys are StoreStash keys under 0.2.0's rule (one press runs one key).
- **Pointing at a set-up chest** says what it takes (a setting, on); a setting shows the setup key there too (off). After a quick stack, a line can say which set-up chest took what (off).
- **The setup is kept in the chest's own record**, beside Reserve Stock's mark, written as its owner and sent at once: friends on the pack see it and follow it; a friend without the pack sees an ordinary chest. Carts' and ships' chests can be set up (a setting, on). A setup from a newer version of the pack (or longer than a setup can be) is kept as it is and takes nothing.
- **In F1** each StoreStash feature sits behind a row that opens and closes it (Favorites, Personal Stacker, Sorting, Trash, Chest setups), with shudnal's ConfigurationManager 1.1.22 or later; with another manager they all show open.
- **The quick stack itself:** a chest reserved while its claim waited no longer holds back what it was meant to take for that press: the stack plans again without it at once.
- Twenty-one new settings, in the section "Chest setups"; "Chest setups" off makes the quick stack ignore every setup (they stay in the chests). Out of the box nothing changes until a chest is set up.
- The chest service (in every mod of the pack that carries it) follows setups in its routing (`ChestSetup`, `StackRules.Setups`, set-up chests first); the catalogue gains `ItemPicks`.

## 0.3.0 (2026-09-30)

- **Sorting** (card A53, job 32), replacing HexQuickStackStorage's, written from scratch: S (inventory open) or a button below the weight box sorts your inventory by the pack's own order (Add to Cart's: families, their groups, then progression), merging part-stacks; the hotbar, what you wear and your favorites stay in place (settings), and what you wear or hold never merges; EquipmentAndQuickSlots' rows are never touched. A chest is sorted as it opens, once it's yours (every chest, only yours, or off), and by a Sort button left of Place stacks; never a grave, and no chest while MultiUserChest is loaded. Other orders: by type, name, weight, value.
- **The trash can** (card A53): Left Alt + right-click marks an item's kind as trash, with a red border; the can above the armour box deletes every marked stack in your inventory after asking ("Don't ask again"), never the hotbar, what you wear or hold, favorites or quest items; carrying an item, it deletes just that. Your HexQuickStackStorage trash marks are taken in once.
- **S is one of StoreStash's keys** under 0.2.0's rule (one press runs one key: the one with the most modifiers held), and no StoreStash key works while the trash can's question is up.
- A chest someone else marked as theirs (0.2.0's mark) isn't sorted as it opens, unless a setting says so; its Sort button still sorts it.
- Sorting and the trash each have their own switch and section of settings.

## 0.2.0 (2026-09-30)

- **Your personal chests** (card A43, built into StoreStash at miikeskii's pick): **H** (a setting) quick stacks into your personal chests nearby only: the game's Personal Chests you built, and chests you marked as yours; every chest you built counts too with a setting (off); a chest someone else marked never does. The quick stack's own settings apply (radius, hotbar, trophies, favorites, the message); what goes in follows "Only into chests that already hold the item" unless its own setting says only what they hold, or anything.
- **Left Alt + H** marks the chest you point at (or have open) as yours, or takes your mark off. The mark is kept in the chest's own record, written as its owner and sent at once; it names the character; only whoever set it can take it off. "Yours", or whose it is, shows when you point at a marked chest.
- **StoreStash's quick stack leaves a chest someone else marked alone** (a setting, on), in every game running it: read from the chest itself by the pack's chest service. Place stacks and holding E at that chest still work. A friend still on HexQuickStackStorage isn't kept out (StoreStash stays off for them).
- **"Use my personal chests"** now also covers chests you marked: off, P leaves to H your Personal Chests and marked chests, each kind while H counts it (never every chest you built).
- **A personal quick stack button** (off at first): with P's on too, it sits below P's, the two sharing the space between the armour and weight boxes at 32 px; alone, it takes P's place.
- One press never runs two keys: Left Alt + H marks, and doesn't also run H.
- The buttons make the game's click sound from their very first click.
- Twelve new settings, in the section "Personal Stacker".
- The chest service (in every mod of the pack that carries it) gains the "mine" mark (`ChestMine`) and reads it where a quick stack chooses its chests.

## 0.1.0 (2026-09-30)

- **First version**, as a mod of miikeskii's QoL Pack and on its own: the pack's own quick stack and favorites, replacing HexQuickStackStorage's, written from scratch.
- **P** (a setting) or a button beside the inventory puts what you carry into the chests nearby (150 m) that already hold it, part-stacks first; trophies go to any chest holding a trophy; never while you type, or while F1's window is open.
- **Place stacks and holding E** at a chest follow the same rules (settings), only into what the chest already holds, as the game's own.
- **Favorites:** Left Shift + right-click marks an item's kind, with a gold border; favorites stay with you. Your HexQuickStackStorage favorites are taken in once.
- **The hotbar:** unfavorited items go by default (card A18); a setting keeps it, or lets everything but what you wear go.
- **Safe with friends:** every chest is asked of its owner first, read again, added to before your stack is taken from, and held a moment after, through the pack's chest service.
- **Stays off while HexQuickStackStorage is installed,** and says so.
