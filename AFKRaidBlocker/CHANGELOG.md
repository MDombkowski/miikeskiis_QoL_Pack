# Changelog

## 0.1.0 (2026-10-10)

- **First version**, as a mod of miikeskii's QoL Pack and on its own (card A98, his name).
- **Away** after 5 minutes without moving, a key, the mouse or a gamepad (1 to 60 minutes, or never; or movement only), or at once when sitting or lying down by an emote (sit, rest and relax by default, counted while the game holds their pose; any emote can be listed); a chair and a bed can count too, and sitting or lying can wait for the same minutes. **Back**, away by the minutes, at the first movement or input that counts (a key or button as it's pressed, the mouse once it really moves); sitting or lying, on standing up. Keys and the mouse count only while the game's window has the focus. Dying doesn't bring you back; loading into a world starts you out there.
- **While away, and while nobody within 96 m (a setting) is still playing, raids aren't started on you:** your report to the server says no base (raids that need a base, the default) or no raids ready (every random event), only as it's sent, and in single player or a world you host only as your own game reads it before a roll. Nothing else in your game changes; your character's record tells other players' copies of the mod whether you're away (and whether you show the mark). A player near you who is still playing, or who doesn't have the mod, gets raids as usual, and they can start on you (the players your game has loaded, up to 128 m, and anyone farther who shares their map position).
- A line on screen going away and coming back (middle, top left or none); lines in the log (off at first).
- An away mark beside your name over your head for friends with the mod (off at first), and theirs on your screen (on).
- On for everyone out of the box, at his call.
