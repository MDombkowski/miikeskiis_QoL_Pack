# Changelog

## 0.2.0 (2026-10-11)

- **Lists picked from pictures**: "Metal bars", "Always repairs" and "Never repairs" have a Pick button in the settings window (F1): it opens a window of everything the list can hold, each with its picture, where a click puts a thing on the list or takes it off. The typed box stays beside it, and the list is written as the mod reads it, with any names the game doesn't know kept. The window is set in the new section "Lists (every mod)" (its size, its pictures', whether things not found yet show).

## 0.1.0 (2026-10-11)

- **First version**, as a mod of miikeskii's QoL Pack and on its own (his idea and his name).
- **The Grinding Wheel and the Smith's Anvil repair at a press of Use (E):** everything in your inventory, worn or not, that each one covers, to full and in one go; the Grinding Wheel non-metallic gear and the Smith's Anvil metallic gear out of the box, either set to metallic, non-metallic or all. Metallic gear is gear whose recipe takes a metal bar from the "Metal bars" list (the Forge of Potential's idols never count). No station level is needed. Their hover text says what each repairs.
- **Both can be built with no Forge near**; a Workbench is still needed to build them, and 2 m of room from any other station extension.
- **The Sharpening Stone repairs in the field:** its hotbar key, or a right-click in your inventory, runs one bar (a second per item, or a flat time) in the game's own action bar, then gives each worn item back a share of its full durability (50% by default), never past full. Which gear it mends (tools, weapons, armour, shields, other; metallic or not; always or never by name) and what it costs (one stone, or a share of a stone's durability per use or per item, the stone then wearing like a tool with its own bar, in a cell of its own: a worn stone never stacks) are settings. A second press, an attack, a sprint, a jump or a dodge stops it, and so can moving or being staggered; a stopped repair keeps the stone, or uses it, by setting. The equip motion, the crafting pose or nothing while it runs.
- **Repairs near stations** (off at first): everything a crafting station can mend when you open it, and once each time you come within 4 m of a station, a Grinding Wheel or a Smith's Anvil.
- Every repair raises the Crafting skill as the game's own repair does (a setting).
