## 0.2.0 (2026-10-10)

- **Shared keys aren't clashes** (his idea). Every use of a key now has a "when": where it works (the world, the inventory, a chest open, the map, the build menu, a menu, while typing, a mod's window), what must be in hand (the hammer, a terrain tool, anything but a build tool) and what it must point at (a chest, an armour stand, a fire, a station). Two uses of one key are a **clash** (red) only when both can work at the same moment: one press does both. Two uses that never can (Left Alt + E at a chest for Reserve Stock, at an armour stand for Outfitter, at a fire or a station for Topper-Upper) are **shared**, in a colour of their own (purple), and listed under "Shared Keys" beside "Clashes".
- **The rule, in one place:** a key of the world fires while you point at something, so it meets any key that needs something pointed at; a tool in hand and a thing pointed at hold together, except a build tool (the game points at nothing while one is in hand); the hammer and the hoe never are in hand at once; the inventory and the world never show at once, but a chest open and the split window do; the build menu and the radial menu sit over the world, so a key of the world works with them open unless it says it doesn't. Two settings of the same mod on one key that can fire at once are a clash too (his words: "two mods (or the same mod)"); a mod's plain key gives way to its own more specific one only where its code picks one per press (StoreStash's keys, Outfitter's stand keys) or reads it strictly; and the game's Use carried under a pack key made to take it (Reserve Stock's Left Alt + E at a chest) isn't a clash.
- **Every when read from the code:** the pack's own keys from each module's key check, the game's buttons and fixed keys from the game's code (1.0.17), and the keys of the mods installed on his setup on 2026-10-10 from theirs (studied, not copied). Another mod's key whose when isn't known counts as working anywhere, or as maybe a clash (amber), as you choose.
- **Hover** names each user with its when, and for a clash which two fire at once.
- **Beside F1 while you pick a key**, the panel judges every key for that setting's own when: a key used only where the setting never works shows purple, free for it; one taken where it works shows as used. The same in the game's Controls tab for the button being rebound.
- **New settings:** "Keys shared by places never true at once" (shown as shared, shown as used, or counted as clashes), "A key whose when isn't known counts as" (anywhere, or maybe), "Whens for other mods' keys" (your own lines, which win over Spare Keys'), "Show the when on hover", and "Colour: shared".
- **The game's table, corrected from its code:** the Tab and Left Shift buttons and the keyboard's camera zoom are read by nothing and are left out; E, Q and the mouse buttons each work with or without a build tool as the game reads them; Ctrl + F3 and F9 work in any screen; the split window sits over the inventory.

## 0.1.0 (2026-10-09)

- **First version**, as a mod of miikeskii's QoL Pack and on its own (his name for it).
- **The key map** (Insert, or the button in F1): a picture of the keyboard (full, without a number pad, or a laptop's) and the mouse, every key coloured free, free alone but taken with a modifier (a C, A or S in its corner), used, a clash, or maybe used; the line under it names who uses the key under the pointer and where, and suggests up to three free keys for one in use. A row None · Ctrl · Alt · Shift shows the keys with a modifier held (left click the left key, right click the right one) and follows a modifier you hold. The list under it, browsed the pack's way: Everything (by state), Free Keys (by where they sit), Clashes (by what's held), Maybe Used, The Game (by where its keys work), Console Binds, the pack (by mod) and each mod (by its settings' sections), keyboard order inside each heading, a search box whose hits the counts follow, and a click on a key to list only its rows. Small, Medium or Large, and an opacity for its background.
- **What it reads, read only:** the game's buttons with your rebinds and Jötunn's buttons (the game's own table), the keys the game reads in its code (a table checked against its code), your console binds, every loaded mod's KeyCode and KeyboardShortcut settings (and text settings named like a key whose value is one, as maybe used), the pack's own window keys, and, once per session off the game's main thread, the fixed keys in every other mod's code (Mono.Cecil). Worked out again only when a setting, a bind or debug mode changes, or as the window or a panel opens.
- **The rules:** a key counts with exactly its own modifiers held, and with more held when "A plain key counts as used under any modifier" says so (Always out of the box: the game's keys fire whatever else is held); two users clash when they belong to different mods and their places can meet (at a chest and at an armour stand never do; the inventory and the world never do); Shift, Ctrl and Alt are never called a clash; a mod's own more specific key wins over its plain one; a pack key made to take the game's key there (Reserve Stock's Left Alt + E at a chest) isn't a clash with it.
- **While picking a key:** a display-only panel beside shudnal's configuration manager's window while one of its key settings waits for a key (following a modifier you hold), and one in the game's Controls tab while it rebinds. They take no clicks: a click there would be the new key.
- Keys the game can't read (F13 to F15, Help, SysReq, the shifted characters) are never drawn or asked about. Leaves Insert to Bindrune when it's installed; reads ComfyMods' Shortcuts' keys from its settings.
- **Also:** a Jötunn button with no setting behind it (XPortal's Up and Down Arrow, read only while its portal panel shows) counts as maybe used, not as a clash; Jötunn's gamepad side of a button ("Joy!…") is read right; the key map opens only in a world (the main menu reads its own keys behind any window); the F1 panel goes clear of whichever of the manager's windows waits for the key (its Edit window too), and says when blue isn't safe (a KeyCode setting, or one of the pack's keys: they fire with more modifiers held); "may still fire" for another mod's shortcut counted under more modifiers; the list's narrow columns fit their words; StoreStash's and Outfitter's keys count only while their switch is on; the F1 button greys out where it can't open the map, and closes BepInEx's own manager too; Spare Keys' own Insert isn't counted while it's left to Bindrune; the map and its views are made again only when a use really changed, and the list's rows only when what they show did; a mod assembly over 20 MB isn't read for keys.
