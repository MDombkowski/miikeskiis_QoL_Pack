# Add to Cart

Pick what you want to build, craft or take from a window of thumbnails, and one click fetches it from your chests into your inventory. Fill a cart with how many of each (two portals, not just one; 300 resin), see what it needs and what the chests can give, then press Fetch: it takes what you're short, from the chests near you, safely even when a friend owns them.

## What it does

- **A window with three tabs.** Press **K** (a setting). The window comes in three sizes (a setting): Large (the default), Normal and Extra large, with sharp text at each.
  - **Build:** every building piece your character knows, from every build tool (hammer, hoe …). What isn't a building is left out: the hoe's terrain actions (Raise Ground, Paved Road), the serving tray's food placements, and the cultivator's plantings unless "List plantings" is on. Comfort pieces show their comfort in the top-left corner.
  - **Craft:** every recipe you know, and what every processing station you can build makes: the Fermenter's meads and potions (from their bases), the smelter's, blast furnace's and kiln's bars and coal (with the smelter's coal), the windmill's flour, the spinning wheel's thread, the eitr refinery's eitr, the cooking stations' and the oven's food. A thumbnail that makes more than one shows how many (×20, ×6).
  - **Take:** every item in the chests in range, each shown once with how many there are (bottom left). Taking puts the item itself in your cart: a stew, not its ingredients; 300 resin, not the tree.
- **One way to browse, on every tab.**
  - **The list on the left picks what shows:** All, your favorites and your lists, then the tab's own. Click one of its orange headings (Your Lists, Kinds, Made At …) to fold that whole section away, and again to bring it back; folded headings are kept with your character. Build: the game's categories (Building Structures opens to Flooring, Walls, Roofing, Stairs, Doors and Windows, Architecture) and materials (Wood, Stone, Metal, Hides and Fabrics, Other, each opening to its members: Materials › Finewood lists every piece costing finewood, as the game's build menu does). Craft: the kinds (Materials, Tools, Weapons …) and Made At, the stations in the order you can first build them. Take: the families (Gear, Wood, Stone and Minerals, Metals, Food …), each opening to its members. Only what has something in it shows, with how many; a family opens with a click, or its arrow.
  - **Group** makes the headings. Its default, **Auto**, follows the pick and says what it does, "Auto (Material)": walls by what they're made of, weapons by type, tools by use, a family by its members. It offers only choices that would change the view, such as **Equipment Slot** where gear shows (Head, Shoulders, Chest, Legs …) and **Preparation** where food shows (Simple Foods, Prepared Dishes, Cooking Ingredients), and **None**.
  - **Sort** orders things inside each heading: **Progression** by default (Meadows to the Deep North, then the game's own order), Name, and where they would change something: Health, Stamina, Eitr or Duration for food (the number shows in each tile's corner), Count (on Take how many the chests hold, elsewhere how many you could make), Recent and Frequent (your fetches).
  - **First** puts your favorites among what's shown at the top, under a heading of their own.
  - **The line above the results** says what is shown and how many; **Show All ×** goes back to everything.
  - **The filter** (press **F**) searches the whole tab: names, what things cost, categories and families, biomes and stations. Every word must match, so "stone floor" works. The list on the left then shows where the hits are, and a pick there narrows the search. When Take finds nothing, it says where a chest near you holds something of that name and why the cart leaves it out (out of range, someone else has the chest open, a private chest, the last one kept back …).
  - Each tab remembers its Group and Sort (a setting), with your character.
- **Your favorites and lists are the game's own.** **Middle-click** a thumbnail to star it; a small checklist of your lists opens, with **Add List** and **Remove from Favorites**. Your lists are the build menu's favorite categories: a list made here shows there, and the other way round, and a build piece starred in either is starred in both. Items and recipes are starred too (one star per thing: Lox Meat Pie starred on Craft is starred on Take), kept in the same place with your character; a friend's game without the mod keeps them and shows nothing odd. **Add List…** on the left makes a new list; **right-click** a list to rename it; delete one in the game's build menu (middle-click it there), which asks first.
- **Clicks on a thumbnail:**
  - **Left** adds one; **Shift** adds 5 (on Take, a full stack); **Ctrl** adds as many as the chests allow.
  - **Right** takes one away (with Shift, 5 or a stack).
  - **Middle** stars it and opens your lists.
  - **Alt**, on Take: finds the chests (Locate).
- **Hover** over a thumbnail to see its picture and what one costs as "X/Y": X what one takes, Y what the chests in range hold. X turns red when that's what the chests can't cover. On Take it says how many the chests hold and how many you carry.
- **A cart.** In the cart, **−**, **+** and **×** change a line, or type a number into its box; **right-click** anywhere on a line to take it out. The cart stays until a fetch has covered all of it, or you press Clear.
- **A running preview.** For each material: what you have of what the cart needs, what the chests will give, and what's still short. Below it: your weight after the fetch against what you can carry, and how many free slots it needs against what you have. Red means too heavy, or not enough room.
- **Greyed out means the chests can't cover one more.** A thumbnail turns grey and fades (settings pick the look and how faded) when the chests can't give what one more of it needs, after the rest of the cart. On Build and Craft, click it anyway and it asks **"Add all available?"**, naming what's short; Yes adds it, and the fetch then takes all there is.
- **Fetch.** Takes from the nearest chests first exactly what the preview shows under "chests", and says what it got, in the window and in the corner of the screen.
- **Locate** (on Take): press Locate, then click an item (or Alt+click it). A label floats over every chest in range holding it: the item, how many, how far. You see it through walls and hills, and only you see it. Opening a chest clears its label (or, with a setting, every label). **Stop locating** clears them all. Handy for tidying your storage without opening every chest.
- **The mouse wheel** scrolls the window's lists two rows a notch (a setting).

## Safe with friends

Only the game of the player who "owns" a chest can change it for everyone; a change made anywhere else is quietly undone, which is how other chest mods lose or copy items. So before taking anything, Add to Cart asks each chest's owner for it with the game's own "open" request, the one your E key sends, which every player's game answers, with or without mods. If a friend has that chest open, the answer is no and the chest is skipped. Once the chest is yours, it reads what the chest really holds right then, puts each item into your inventory first, and takes from the chest only what arrived. If a skipped chest's items can be found in another chest, it looks there too. For three seconds after taking, each chest it took from says "in use" to anyone else who tries it, until their game has caught up with what it now holds.

## Good to know

- **Only you need it.** It's client-side: neither the server nor the other players need it.
- **Which chests:** those within **150 m** (a setting) that you could open by hand: your own, and a friend's public chests, never a private one or one behind someone else's ward. Carts and ships count, but not a ship someone else is aboard (taking from it would hand you the ship). Chests the world made (dungeon loot), gravestones and other mods' kinds of chest (PlanBuild's plan totems) never do. A setting limits it to chests you built. Only chests your game has loaded around you can be reached, about 128 to 190 m at the game's default settings.
- **Reserved chests are left alone**, ready for miikeskii's Reserve Stock mod (not out yet): a reserved chest is never taken from, and its contents are counted apart.
- **Leaves one of each stackable item in every chest,** so quick stack still knows where things belong; never a lone sword or a single piece of armour. By default it follows AzuCraftyBoxes' own "Leave One Item" setting when that mod is installed.
- **Build and Craft take only what you're short** of what you carry already. Turn "Take only what I'm short" off to take their full amounts. Take always takes exactly the number in the cart.
- **You still build, craft and process as usual.** Fetching doesn't place, craft or cook anything, and doesn't change which station you need nearby.
- **Your favorites, lists, fetch history** (for Recent and Frequent) **and each tab's Group and Sort** are kept with your character. The favorites and lists are the game's own, in the same place as the build menu's.
- **Seasonal pieces and recipes** (the Yule tree, the Jack-o-turnip, the Midsummer crown …) show while they're in season, as in the game.
- **Where each thing sits** (its family, kind, biome, what a piece is made of) is shared by all miikeskii's mods, from one place in the bundle, so they sort and group alike. Things from other mods fall into place by their type: weapons by skill, armour by slot, the rest under Other; nothing is hidden.
- **Not in this version:** recipes that take any one of several ingredients, and upgrades.
- **It is also part of miikeskii's QoL Pack,** the bundle of all miikeskii's Valheim mods. With the bundle installed, this separate copy stands aside and does nothing, and says so in the log; you can remove it.

## Requirements

- BepInExPack for Valheim
- Jötunn, the Valheim library

## Installing

- **With Thunderstore Mod Manager or r2modman:** Settings, then "Import local mod from file", and choose `drumcowski-AddToCart-<version>.zip`. Keep the author `drumcowski` and the name `AddToCart` as the import fills them in, so that a newer zip later replaces this one instead of installing beside it.
- **By hand:** copy `AddToCart.dll` into a folder named `drumcowski-AddToCart` inside `BepInEx\plugins`. With a mod manager, `BepInEx` is inside your profile's folder.

## Settings

Open the settings in game with a configuration manager (shudnal's ConfigurationManager opens with F1). They are kept in `BepInEx\config\modprojects.addtocart.cfg`, which appears after the first start; in the bundle, in the section "Add to Cart" of its own file. Every change counts at once.

| Setting | Default | What it does |
|---|---|---|
| Enabled | on | Off closes the window at once, clears any Locate labels, and its key does nothing; on brings the key back. |
| Key to open the cart | K | Opens and closes the window. Escape closes it too. |
| Chest range (metres) | 150 | How far away a chest may be. 5 to 200. |
| Which chests | AnyChestICanOpen | Or OnlyChestsIBuilt. |
| Leave one of each item in chests | FollowAzuCraftyBoxes | As AzuCraftyBoxes' "Leave One Item" says (leave one if it isn't installed), or Always, or Never. Stackable items only. |
| Take only what I'm short | on | Build and Craft. Off: takes their full amounts, whatever you carry. |
| List plantings | off | On: Build also lists the cultivator's crops and saplings (one seed each), to fetch the seeds for a field. |
| Window size | Large | Large (a quarter bigger), Normal (as 0.3.0 drew it) or ExtraLarge (half as big again). Large and ExtraLarge are kept to what fits your screen: on a 16:9 screen at the game's default GUI scale, ExtraLarge comes out about 38% bigger; with the game's GUI scale set higher, the window starts bigger and less is added. The text stays sharp at every size. |
| Thumbnails the chests can't cover | GreyAndFaded | GreyAndFaded: grey and faded to the opacity below. Grey: grey, not faded. Faded: faded to the opacity below, in colour. |
| Opacity of thumbnails the chests can't cover | 0.1 | How much of such a thumbnail still shows, with GreyAndFaded or Faded: 0.05 to 0.9. |
| Favorites first | on | Your favorites at the top of what's shown; the window's First switch changes it too. |
| Scroll speed (rows per notch) | 2 | How far one notch of the mouse wheel scrolls the lists. 0.5 to 10. |
| Expandable families | on | Families (and Building Structures) open in the list on the left to show their members. Off: only the families show, and their members appear as headings when one is picked. |
| Remember each tab's group and sort | on | Each tab keeps its own Group and Sort, while you play and between sessions (with your character). Off: every tab opens on the defaults below. |
| Default group | Auto | Auto, Biome or None. |
| Default sort | Progression | Progression or Name. |
| Build category order, Build material order, Craft kind order, Craft station order, Take family order | miikeskii's orders | One line each, names separated by commas; a family's members follow it in brackets, "Metals (Copper, Tin, Bronze …)". Names left out go at the end, and names the mod doesn't know are ignored, so a typo can't hide anything. Empty: the default. |
| Locate: opening one chest clears every label | off | On: when several chests hold the item, opening any one clears every label. |

0.3.0 retired the setting "Sort by": each tab now keeps its own Group and Sort. Its line may stay in an older settings file, where it does nothing.

## If something goes wrong

Look in `BepInEx\LogOutput.log` for lines from "Add to Cart": every fetch writes what it got there too, and the first time the window opens it says what it lists and what it leaves out. If the game has changed so that the mod can't find what it needs, it switches itself off and says why, and no chest is touched. The same goes for an error while playing.

## Building from source

- **Build:** `dotnet build -c Release` in this folder. The result is `bin\Release\net472\AddToCart.dll`.
- **Package:** `tools\package.ps1` checks that the project file, `src\Module.cs` and this changelog agree on the version, builds, and writes `dist\drumcowski-AddToCart-<version>.zip`. Until Add to Cart is released on its own, its zip carries the bundle's icon (`..\QoLMods\icon.png`).
- **What's where:** the mod's code is in `src\` (the bundle compiles it from here too), its own plugin in `standalone\`; `kit\` is a copy of the QoL Mods kit that runs it, and `parts\Chests\` and `parts\Catalogue\` copies of the chest service and the catalogue (where each thing sits, and the browsing rules) it shares with other mods of the bundle. How a module and a part are made: `..\QoLMods\MODULES.md`.
- **References:** it compiles against the game's DLLs and the BepInEx, HarmonyX and Jötunn DLLs where they are already installed. Nothing is downloaded and nothing is copied or written outside this folder. Override the two paths if yours are elsewhere: `-p:ValheimManaged="<Valheim>\valheim_Data\Managed" -p:ModProfile="<mod manager profile folder>"`.
