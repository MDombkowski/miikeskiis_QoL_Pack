# Changelog

## 0.1.0 (2026-09-25)

- **First version**, as a mod of drumcowski's QoL Mods and on its own.
- The mouse wheel zooms the big map from fully in to fully out in **18 notches** instead of the game's 44 or so, and back in the same number. The setting "Scroll notches, fully in to fully out" (4 to 60) changes it at once.
- **Zooms toward the mouse pointer**: the place under the pointer stays under it. The setting "Zoom toward the mouse pointer" turns it off, and the wheel then zooms around the middle of the view, as the game does.
- The comma and period keys, a gamepad and the small map zoom as they always did.
- Not yet tested in game.
